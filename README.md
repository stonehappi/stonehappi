## 📍 About Me

I'm a Cambodian lecturer and researcher, currently a **PhD candidate in Management Technology** at the Sirindhorn International Institute of Technology (SIIT), Thammasat University. My work sits at the intersection of **artificial intelligence and operations management** — applying reinforcement learning, genetic algorithms, and heuristic optimization to real-world **production scheduling**, forecasting, and energy-efficiency problems.

I also love building software, and enjoy turning research ideas into working systems.
